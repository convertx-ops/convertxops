What actually happens on the first call with our AI agent?

As a solar and roofing business owner, you're likely juggling a lot of responsibilities. You may wonder, "Do I really need an AI voice agent to handle all these calls? Is this worth my time?" Let’s break down what actually happens during the first call and why it’s worth considering.

Let's say you operate a roofing business and you get 20 inquiries per week. If you miss out on 40% of those calls, you’re missing out on 8 potential clients. That’s a significant loss.

Now, imagine you have a team of salespeople, but they're spread thin. They might take weeks to respond or even ignore the calls. In this scenario, a missed 40% of inquiries translates to a missed opportunity of 8 clients. Each of these clients could represent thousands of dollars in potential revenue.

Our AI voice agent is designed to address these missed opportunities efficiently. On the first call, the AI agent would greet you, introduce itself, and quickly identify if the prospect is a good fit based on the parameters set by your business, such as roof type, service area, timeline, and budget.

If it's a good fit, the AI agent would qualify the prospect by asking the right questions. It would provide a quick response, saving you time. If the prospect needs more information, the AI agent can guide them to a live human salesperson who can then escalate the conversation or book the estimate on your calendar.
