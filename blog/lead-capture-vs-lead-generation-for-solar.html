Lead capture vs lead generation for solar

Solar business owners often face the challenge of converting potential customers into loyal clients. Two crucial strategies in this process are lead capture and lead generation. Lead generation involves attracting potential customers through various marketing channels such as social media, online ads, and email marketing. For example, a roofing business can create an engaging email series explaining the benefits of solar installations, which serves as lead generation.

Lead capture, on the other hand, focuses on converting leads who have shown interest into actual customers. Once potential customers have engaged with these initial marketing efforts, businesses can capture them by offering a free consultation or quote. This step is crucial because it turns a mere interest into a concrete step towards making a purchase.

For instance, if a roofing business generated 500 leads through their lead generation efforts, they can expect to convert around 20% of those leads into paying customers. This means they might end up with approximately 100 new solar installation customers, which is a realistic figure and a significant number for any solar and roofing business owner aiming to grow their customer base.
