Lead capture vs lead generation for solar

Solar businesses often focus on lead generation and lead capture to grow their customer base. Lead generation is the process of finding potential customers, while lead capture involves collecting information from these leads. For instance, a roofing company might generate leads by attending local trade shows or posting ads online. Lead capture then comes into play by setting up a form on their website where visitors can sign up for a free consultation or receive more information about their services.

A realistic example is that a roofing company generated 500 leads through a series of online ads over the course of a quarter. They then used these leads effectively by capturing 100 of them with lead capture forms. This approach not only helps in maintaining contact with interested parties but also gives them more data to target their marketing efforts more effectively.
