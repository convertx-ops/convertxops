How much money does one missed roofing call actually cost you?

When you run a roofing or solar business, one of the most critical aspects of your operations is converting leads into sales. A missed opportunity can be costly, especially when you consider how much time and effort you put into nurturing a lead and closing a deal. Let’s put some numbers to the cost of missing out on a potential sale.

Imagine you get 20 inquiries per week. That’s a good average for a busy roofing or solar business. If you’re managing this manually, you might miss 40% of these calls, which means 8 inquiries get missed. Now, let’s assume these missed calls are due to not having an automated system in place, and you lose out on the sale.

If a typical roofing project costs around $5,000 to $10,000, we can use a midpoint of $7,500 for our calculations. If you miss out on just one of these calls, the potential lost sale is $7,500. That’s a significant chunk of money, especially when you consider that you’re not just losing the sale but also the potential for referrals and future business from that client.

Multiply that by the 8 missed calls, and the potential lost revenue climbs to $60,000 in one year. In terms of your bottom line, how many weeks of your monthly or annual budget does that represent? It’s a substantial figure that underscores the importance of having a robust system in place to handle your leads efficiently.
