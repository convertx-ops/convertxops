Will an AI voice agent sound robotic to my customers?

Dear solar and roofing business owners out there,

I often hear from you that you are skeptical about whether your business even needs an AI voice agent. But let’s take a moment to break down some numbers that might shift your mindset.

Imagine this: You get 20 new leads every week. If your team can only qualify one in five calls, that means you’re losing 4 out of 5 leads. In a year, that’s 52 opportunities you’re not pursuing. Multiply that by the cost of a solar installation, which can range from $20,000 to $100,000, and you’re looking at a potential loss of $1,000,000 to $5,000,000 in revenue.
